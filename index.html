About Me

I’m a security researcher and penetration tester focused on web security, Active Directory, and hands-on lab work. My write-ups are built to help future me, but I hope they help others too.

I like learning by breaking things, documenting how they work, and sharing the path from recon to remediation. I spend time on HackTheBox, PortSwigger Labs, and AD lab setups.
